About us

Industry experience, brought back to the classroom.

Rajeev Kumar's Knowledge World is an MSME-registered education company from Tiruvuru, Andhra Pradesh, with two verticals: ILP and Gati Academy.

Founder

Rajeev Kumar Garikapati

Cloud & Data Solutions Architect · Founder of ILP

11+ years building and running systems on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, with Python and data at the core. AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner.

Rajeev started ILP to close the gap between what colleges teach and what companies expect, by teaching the way work is actually done: real tools, small steps and real projects.

What we believe

Practice beats theory. Students learn by typing and running code, not by watching.

Small wins build confidence. One clear step at a time, so every student finishes.

Honesty first. We promise skills and readiness, never shortcuts or guaranteed placements.

Listen every day. Daily student feedback shapes the next session.
